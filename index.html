Our Story Est. 2019 · Single Origin

We roast in small batches because coffee is a crop, not a commodity.

Amber Hour started as a Sunday pop-up on a borrowed espresso machine. Six years on, we still buy direct from three family farms, roast every lot in-house in batches under 12kg, and cup each one before it ever reaches a menu.

The name comes from the last hour of golden light before a farm's harvest ends for the day — the hour growers say gives the sweetest cherries. We try to bottle a little of that unhurried light into every cup we pour.
